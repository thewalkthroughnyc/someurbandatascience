# Walk No. 04 — route

**Start: the corner of Seneca Ave and Palmetto St.** ~90 min total. End at
Abracadabra Magic Cafe for the discussion. Rain date: **Sat Oct 3**, same time,
same start.

Seneca Ave sits in the middle of the M-only cluster (Central Av → Knickerbocker
Av → Seneca Av → Forest Av → Fresh Pond Rd → Middle Village-Metropolitan Av,
with the Myrtle-Wyckoff transfer complex in between Knickerbocker and Seneca).
Within the cluster the data shows rent runs highest at the Bushwick/Ridgewood
end (Central Av, ~$2,300 median) and falls going deeper into Queens (Forest Av,
Fresh Pond Rd, ~$1,900). Four teams, four directions.

## The four groups

**Everyone walks 15 minutes out, then turns around.** Time-boxed, not
destination-boxed — the landmarks below are bearings, not targets anyone has to
reach. ~30 min walking, ~35 with stops.

| Group | Route | Bearing | ~15 min reaches | Expectation (from the data) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| North | Palmetto → right on Woodward → left on Putnam | Fresh Pond Rd (M) | just short of it (15.8 min) | **falling** — $2,083 → $1,913 |
| South | West on Seneca → left on Gates Ave | Knickerbocker Av (M) | about there (15.4 min) | **rising, then flat** — $2,083 → $2,116 |
| West | Seneca → Grover Cleveland Park → right on Hart → the intersections | — | ~13 min per map read | open — no data prediction; the live experiment |
| East | East along Seneca | Evergreen Park | ~15 min per map read | open — same test, other side |

Three predictions each, by the clock rather than by building: one in the first
minute, one at minutes 7–10, one at minutes 12–15 just before turning around. No
anchor given beyond "Ridgewood is an up-and-coming neighborhood."

Holding walk-time constant across all four groups is the point: **direction
becomes the only thing that varies.** It also means the east and west endpoints
never need measuring — whatever they reach in 15 minutes is the data point.
