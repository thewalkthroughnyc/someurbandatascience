# Reading List — Curb Economics / Value of Parking Space

Research for The Walkthrough NYC, Math Walk 5: the potential economic value of a block's curb if parking spaces were repurposed (dining, bike parking, loading, parklets, green infrastructure, etc.).

Every entry below has its PDF in this folder, named by the entry's id (e.g. `pierce-shoup-2013.pdf`).

Each entry has the same fields: `title`, `authors`, `year`, `venue`, `type`, `url`, `doi` (where known), `note`.
`type` is one of: `peer-reviewed`, `agency-report`, `research-report`.
Notes are summaries, not quotes. Verify any figure against the original before publishing it.

---

## 1. Start here

### volker-handy-2021
- title: Economic impacts on local businesses of investments in bicycle and pedestrian infrastructure: a review of the evidence
- authors: Volker, J. M. B.; Handy, S. L.
- year: 2021
- venue: Transport Reviews 41(4), 401–431
- type: peer-reviewed
- url: https://doi.org/10.1080/01441647.2021.1912849
- doi: 10.1080/01441647.2021.1912849
- note: Review of 23 US and Canadian studies, 15 of which quantified an economic impact on local businesses. Walking/biking facilities generally had positive or non-significant effects on nearby retail and food businesses; possible negative effect on auto-centric businesses. Best entry point — its bibliography maps the field.

## 2. Does removing parking hurt business?

### arancibia-2019
- title: Measuring the Local Economic Impacts of Replacing On-Street Parking With Bike Lanes: A Toronto (Canada) Case Study
- authors: Arancibia, D.; Farber, S.; Savan, B.; Verlinden, Y.; Smith Lea, N.; Allen, J.; Vernich, L.
- year: 2019
- venue: Journal of the American Planning Association 85(4), 463–481
- type: peer-reviewed
- url: https://doi.org/10.1080/01944363.2019.1638816
- doi: 10.1080/01944363.2019.1638816
- note: Toronto removed 136 on-street spaces on Bloor St for a 2016 pilot bike lane. Case-control, pre-post design surveying merchants and shoppers. Monthly customer spending and customer counts both increased. Strongest design on this exact question.

### clean-air-partnership-2010
- title: Bike Lanes, On-Street Parking and Business Year 2 Report: A Study of Bloor Street in Toronto's Bloor West Village
- authors: Clean Air Partnership
- year: 2010
- venue: Clean Air Partnership (Toronto)
- type: research-report
- url: https://carrefour.vivreenville.org/storage/app/media/publications/Articles-de-fond-Encyclopedique/infrastructure-cyclable/bike-lanes-street-parking-and-business-year-2-report-study-bloor-street-torontos-bloor-west-village.pdf
- note: 21% of visitors arrived by car; 61% of merchants estimated a higher share. Merchant-perception-gap method is replicable on a walk (intercept survey vs. merchant estimate).

## 3. Pricing the curb

### pierce-shoup-2013
- title: Getting the Prices Right: An Evaluation of Pricing Parking by Demand in San Francisco
- authors: Pierce, G.; Shoup, D.
- year: 2013
- venue: Journal of the American Planning Association 79(1), 67–81
- type: peer-reviewed
- url: https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/01944363.2013.787307
- doi: 10.1080/01944363.2013.787307
- pdf_source: http://shoup.bol.ucla.edu/PricingParkingByDemand.pdf (author's preprint — cite page numbers from the JAPA version)
- note: SFpark evaluation. First estimate of on-street parking demand elasticity using measured occupancy. Lit review traces the theory (Vickrey; Arnott & Inci) for pricing curb space.

## 4. NYC-specific

### nycdot-2022-streets-for-recovery
- title: Streets for Recovery: The Economic Benefits of the NYC Open Streets Program
- authors: NYC DOT with Bloomberg Associates
- year: 2022
- venue: NYC DOT
- type: agency-report
- url: https://www.nyc.gov/html/dot/downloads/pdf/streets-for-recovery.pdf
- note: Effects of Open Streets on restaurant/bar sales and survival, March 2020 – August 2021.

### sonkowsky-moss-2022
- title: Open Restaurants in New York
- authors: Sonkowsky, D. T.; Moss, M. L.
- year: 2022
- venue: NYU Wagner Rudin Center
- type: research-report
- url: https://wagner.nyu.edu/files/faculty/publications/Open%20Restaurants%20in%20New%20York.pdf
- note: 12,000+ establishments set up on sidewalks or in parking lanes (~12x the pre-pandemic Sidewalk Café program). Shift toward outer boroughs, majority-nonwhite and lower-income community districts.

### kapshikar-2023
- title: PARK Smarter: Lessons in Curb Pricing for New York City
- authors: Kapshikar, P. (faculty advisor: Donald Shoup)
- year: 2023
- venue: UCLA Institute of Transportation Studies, capstone project (client: NYC DOT Parking Planning and Policy)
- type: research-report
- url: https://escholarship.org/uc/item/58819232
- doi: 10.17610/T6MS5G
- note: Written for NYC DOT as it considers demand-responsive pricing for metered spaces — applies the SFpark/Shoup approach to New York. Bridges sections 3 and 4.

## 5. Curb management — the menu of alternative uses

### itf-2018-shared-use-city
- title: The Shared-Use City: Managing the Curb
- authors: International Transport Forum (OECD)
- year: 2018
- venue: ITF Policy Papers No. 56
- type: research-report
- url: https://www.itf-oecd.org/shared-use-city-managing-curb-0
- doi: 10.1787/3af82772-en
- note: Models alternative curb-use scenarios, their efficiency and revenue implications. Notes cities generally lack curb-use data (a gap walk data could fill).

### jaller-2021-fighting-for-curb-space
- title: Fighting for Curb Space: Parking, Ride-Hailing, Urban Freight Deliveries, and Other Users
- authors: Jaller, M.; Rodier, C.; Zhang, M.; Lin, H.; Lewis, K.
- year: 2021
- venue: UC Davis Institute of Transportation Studies, UCD-ITS-RR-21-08
- type: research-report
- url: https://escholarship.org/uc/item/3jn371hw
- note: Literature review across curb users (pedestrians, bikes, transit, ride-hail, freight) plus a SUMO parking simulation (search, off-street info, double-parking).

## 6. Putting a dollar value on the space itself

### litman-parking-costs
- title: Transportation Cost and Benefit Analysis II — Parking Costs
- authors: Litman, T. (Victoria Transport Policy Institute)
- venue: VTPI
- type: research-report
- url: https://www.vtpi.org/tca/tca0504.pdf
- note: Costs of parking facilities, spaces per vehicle, distribution of costs, internal vs. external.

### litman-comprehensive-parking
- title: Comprehensive Parking Supply, Cost and Pricing Analysis
- authors: Litman, T.
- venue: VTPI
- type: research-report
- url: https://www.vtpi.org/pscp.pdf
- note: Expanded version of the Parking Costs chapter.

### litman-transport-costs-overview
- title: Transportation Costs overview (VTPI)
- authors: Litman, T.
- year: 2022
- venue: VTPI
- type: research-report
- url: https://vtpi.org/tce.pdf
- note: Annualized urban surface parking ~$500–1,500/space (structured ~2x). About ten bikes fit in one car space.

### woudsma-litman-weisbrod-2006
- title: Report on the Estimation of Unit Values of Land Occupied by Transportation Infrastructures
- authors: Woudsma, C.; Litman, T.; Weisbrod, G.
- year: 2006
- venue: Transport Canada
- type: research-report
- url: https://www.vtpi.org/TC_landvalue.pdf
- note: Methods for valuing land occupied by roads and parking — directly relevant to pricing a curb space as land.

1. volker-handy-2021

4. NYC section (section 4)
5. Curb management and land-value sections (5, 6) as needed for the walk's math
