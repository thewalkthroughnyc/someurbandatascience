## 1. Start here

### volker-handy-2021
- title: Economic impacts on local businesses of investments in bicycle and pedestrian infrastructure: a review of the evidence
- authors: Volker, J. M. B.; Handy, S. L.
- year: 2021
- venue: Transport Reviews 41(4), 401–431
- type: peer-reviewed
- url: https://itspubs.ucdavis.edu/publication_detail.php?id=3523
- doi: 10.1080/01441647.2021.1912849
- note: Review of 15 studies. Walking/biking facilities generally had positive or non-significant effects on nearby retail and food businesses; possible negative effect on auto-centric businesses. Best entry point — its bibliography maps the field.

## 2. Does removing parking hurt business?

### arancibia-2019
- title: Measuring the Local Economic Impacts of Replacing On-Street Parking With Bike Lanes: A Toronto (Canada) Case Study
- authors: Arancibia, D., et al. (Clean Air Partnership / University of Toronto team)
- year: 2019
- venue: Journal of the American Planning Association 85(4)
- type: peer-reviewed
- url: https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/01944363.2019.1638816
- doi: 10.1080/01944363.2019.1638816
- note: Toronto removed 136 on-street spaces on Bloor St for a 2016 pilot bike lane. Case-control, pre-post design surveying merchants and shoppers. Monthly customer spending and customer counts both increased. Strongest design on this exact question.

### clean-air-partnership-2009
- title: Bike Lanes, On-Street Parking and Business: A Study of Bloor Street in Toronto's Annex Neighbourhood
- authors: Clean Air Partnership
- year: 2009
- venue: Clean Air Partnership (Toronto)
- type: research-report
- url: https://trid.trb.org/View/1151645
- note: July 2008 survey of 61 merchants and 538 patrons plus parking usage data. Merchants tended to overestimate the share of customers arriving by car.

### clean-air-partnership-2010
- title: Bike Lanes, On-Street Parking and Business Year 2 Report: A Study of Bloor Street in Toronto's Bloor West Village
- authors: Clean Air Partnership
- year: 2010
- venue: Clean Air Partnership (Toronto)
- type: research-report
- url: https://carrefour.vivreenville.org/storage/app/media/publications/Articles-de-fond-Encyclopedique/infrastructure-cyclable/bike-lanes-street-parking-and-business-year-2-report-study-bloor-street-torontos-bloor-west-village.pdf
- note: 21% of visitors arrived by car; 61% of merchants estimated a higher share. Merchant-perception-gap method is replicable on a walk (intercept survey vs. merchant estimate).

### clifton-2013
- title: Examining Consumer Behavior and Travel Choices
- authors: Clifton, K. J.; Muhs, C. D.; et al.
- year: 2013 (report OTREC-RR-12-15)
- venue: Portland State University / OTREC (TREC)
- type: research-report
- url: https://pdxscholar.library.pdx.edu/cengin_fac/145/
- alt_url: https://trec.pdx.edu/research/project/411/Examining_Consumer_Behavior_and_Travel_Choices
- note: 89 businesses in the Portland region. Controlling for demographics, travel mode had no significant effect on spending at convenience stores, bars, restaurants. Bike parking / bike corrals significantly predicted bike mode share.

## 3. Pricing the curb — Shoup and his critics

### shoup-publications
- title: Donald Shoup — Publications
- authors: Shoup, D.
- venue: shoupdogg.com
- type: research-report
- url: https://shoupdogg.com/publications/
- note: Index of Shoup's papers (ACCESS articles, JAPA, Transport Policy). Use for "Cruising for Parking" and related work.

### pierce-shoup-2013
- title: Getting the Prices Right: An Evaluation of Pricing Parking by Demand in San Francisco
- authors: Pierce, G.; Shoup, D.
- year: 2013
- venue: Journal of the American Planning Association 79(1), 67–81
- type: peer-reviewed
- url: https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/01944363.2013.787307
- doi: 10.1080/01944363.2013.787307
- note: SFpark evaluation. First estimate of on-street parking demand elasticity using measured occupancy. Lit review traces the theory (Vickrey; Arnott & Inci) for pricing curb space.

### millard-ball-2014
- title: Is the curb 80% full or 20% empty? Assessing the impacts of San Francisco's parking pricing experiment
- authors: Millard-Ball, A.; Weinberger, R. R.; Hampshire, R. C.
- year: 2014
- venue: Transportation Research Part A: Policy and Practice 63, 76–92
- type: peer-reviewed
- url: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0965856414000470
- note: Methods to infer cruising and probability of finding a space from hourly occupancy data. Finds SFpark reduced cruising ~50%.

### millard-ball-comment-2013
- title: Comment on Pierce and Shoup: Evaluating the Impacts of Performance-Based Parking
- authors: Millard-Ball, A.; Weinberger, R.; Hampshire, R.
- year: 2013
- venue: Journal of the American Planning Association 79(4)
- type: peer-reviewed
- url: https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/01944363.2014.918481
- note: Argues observed occupancy changes may reflect random demand shifts rather than prices; contests methods (endogeneity). Read with pierce-shoup-2013 and the reply below.

### pierce-shoup-response-2013
- title: Response to Millard-Ball et al.: Parking Prices and Parking Occupancy in San Francisco
- authors: Pierce, G.; Shoup, D.
- year: 2013
- venue: Journal of the American Planning Association 79(4)
- type: peer-reviewed
- url: https://www.researchgate.net/publication/270843344_Response_to_Millard-Ball_et_al_Parking_Prices_and_Parking_Occupancy_in_San_Francisco
- note: Pierce & Shoup's reply to the comment.

### japa-editors-note-2014
- title: Editor's Note (on the Pierce & Shoup / Millard-Ball et al. exchange)
- venue: Journal of the American Planning Association
- type: peer-reviewed
- url: https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/01944363.2014.918828
- note: Summarizes the dispute. Useful framing for any before/after curb analysis.

## 4. NYC-specific

### nycdot-2013-sustainable-streets
- title: The Economic Benefits of Sustainable Streets
- authors: NYC Department of Transportation
- year: 2013
- venue: NYC DOT
- type: agency-report
- url: https://nyc.gov/html/dot/downloads/pdf/dot-economic-benefits-of-sustainable-streets.pdf
- note: Sales-tax-based method (with NYC Dept. of Finance) for street-level retail and restaurant sales; seven case studies. Read critically — report concedes difficulty isolating causes; gentrification likely contributed to Vanderbilt Ave results (see CityLab coverage in context section).

### nycdot-2022-streets-for-recovery
- title: Streets for Recovery: The Economic Benefits of the NYC Open Streets Program
- authors: NYC DOT with Bloomberg Associates
- year: 2022
- venue: NYC DOT
- type: agency-report
- url: https://www.nyc.gov/html/dot/downloads/pdf/streets-for-recovery.pdf
- note: Effects of Open Streets on restaurant/bar sales and survival, March 2020 – August 2021.

### sonkowsky-moss-2022
- title: Open Restaurants in New York
- authors: Sonkowsky, D. T.; Moss, M. L.
- year: 2022
- venue: NYU Wagner Rudin Center
- type: research-report
- url: https://wagner.nyu.edu/files/faculty/publications/Open%20Restaurants%20in%20New%20York.pdf
- note: 12,000+ establishments set up on sidewalks or in parking lanes (~12x the pre-pandemic Sidewalk Café program). Shift toward outer boroughs, majority-nonwhite and lower-income community districts.

### ta-nyc25x25-2021
- title: NYC 25x25: A Challenge to New York City's Next Leaders to Give Streets Back to People (Methodology)
- authors: Transportation Alternatives
- year: 2021
- venue: Transportation Alternatives
- type: advocacy-report
- url: https://nyc25x25.org/methodology
- alt_url: https://nyc25x25.org/
- note: ~2.99M on-street spaces, ~85,000 metered (Comptroller figures). Free on-street parking ≈ 20.5% of streetscape area. Good for baseline numbers; check assumptions.

### rpa-2022-right-of-way
- title: Re-Envisioning the Right-of-Way
- authors: Regional Plan Association
- year: 2021–2022
- venue: RPA
- type: advocacy-report
- url: https://rpa.org/work/reports/re-envisioning-right-of-way
- note: Builds on 25x25. Cites TA estimate that demand-based metering of 780,000 spaces could raise $1B+/yr. Covers stormwater, loading zones, phasing out free parking.

### park-smarter-nyc
- title: PARK Smarter: Lessons in Curb Pricing for New York City
- venue: eScholarship (University of California)
- type: research-report
- url: https://escholarship.org/content/qt58819232/qt58819232.pdf
- note: Added while downloading — not on the original list. Confirm authors/year from the PDF.

## 5. Curb management — the menu of alternative uses

### itf-2018-shared-use-city
- title: The Shared-Use City: Managing the Curb
- authors: International Transport Forum (OECD)
- year: 2018
- venue: ITF Policy Papers No. 56
- type: research-report
- url: https://www.itf-oecd.org/shared-use-city-managing-curb-0
- pdf: https://www.itf-oecd.org/sites/default/files/docs/shared-use-city-managing-curb_3.pdf
- doi: 10.1787/3af82772-en
- note: Models alternative curb-use scenarios, their efficiency and revenue implications. Notes cities generally lack curb-use data (a gap walk data could fill).

### jaller-2021-fighting-for-curb-space
- title: Fighting for Curb Space: Parking, Ride-Hailing, Urban Freight Deliveries, and Other Users
- authors: Jaller, M.; Rodier, C.; Zhang, M.; Lin, H.; Lewis, K.
- year: 2021
- venue: UC Davis Institute of Transportation Studies, UCD-ITS-RR-21-08
- type: research-report
- url: https://escholarship.org/uc/item/3jn371hw
- note: Literature review across curb users (pedestrians, bikes, transit, ride-hail, freight) plus a SUMO parking simulation (search, off-street info, double-parking).

### transport-policy-2025-curb-policies
- title: (title to confirm) — review of city curb management policies
- year: 2025
- venue: Transport Policy 164, 196–205
- type: peer-reviewed
- url: https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/trapol/v164y2025icp196-205.html
- note: Finds cities rethinking the curb beyond parking, managing demand for freight, ride-hailing and transit. Confirm title and authors from the link.

### ucla-parklets-2012-2013
- title: Reclaiming the Right-of-Way: A Toolkit for Creating and Implementing Parklets (2012); Evaluation Report: An Assessment of the Spring Street Parklets (2013)
- authors: Loukaitou-Sideris, A.; Brozen, M.; Callahan, C.; Abad Ocubillo, R.; Ocubillo, K.
- year: 2012, 2013
- venue: UCLA Luskin / Lewis Center
- type: toolkit
- url: https://www.lewis.ucla.edu/research/reclaiming-right-of-way-spring-street-parklets
- alt_url: https://innovation.luskin.ucla.edu/urban-greening/complete-and-living-streets/
- note: Toolkit for siting/designing parklets; evaluation of two downtown LA parklets found residents viewed them as an amenity despite lost parking.

## 6. Putting a dollar value on the space itself

### litman-parking-costs
- title: Transportation Cost and Benefit Analysis II — Parking Costs
- authors: Litman, T. (Victoria Transport Policy Institute)
- venue: VTPI
- type: research-report
- url: https://www.vtpi.org/tca/tca0504.pdf
- note: Costs of parking facilities, spaces per vehicle, distribution of costs, internal vs. external.

### litman-comprehensive-parking
- title: Comprehensive Parking Supply, Cost and Pricing Analysis
- authors: Litman, T.
- venue: VTPI
- type: research-report
- url: https://www.vtpi.org/pscp.pdf
- note: Expanded version of the Parking Costs chapter.

### litman-transport-costs-overview
- title: Transportation Costs overview (VTPI)
- authors: Litman, T.
- year: 2022
- venue: VTPI
- type: research-report
- url: https://vtpi.org/tce.pdf
- note: Annualized urban surface parking ~$500–1,500/space (structured ~2x). About ten bikes fit in one car space.

### woudsma-litman-weisbrod-2006
- title: Report on the Estimation of Unit Values of Land Occupied by Transportation Infrastructures
- authors: Woudsma, C.; Litman, T.; Weisbrod, G.
- year: 2006
- venue: Transport Canada
- type: research-report
- url: https://www.vtpi.org/TC_landvalue.pdf
- note: Methods for valuing land occupied by roads and parking — directly relevant to pricing a curb space as land.
