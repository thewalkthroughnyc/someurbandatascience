About

I teach 7th-grade math in Brooklyn. Walkthrough NYC is the same job, done on the sidewalk instead of the classroom.

Every walk starts with a number someone could look up but usually doesn't: a floor-area ratio, a cap rate, a bus headway. I measure the block, work the math in the open, and show what it implies. The math isn't decoration on an opinion — it is the argument.

I'm not a planner, a developer, or an advocate. I'm the person who can take a zoning formula and make it make sense to someone standing next to me on the sidewalk.
